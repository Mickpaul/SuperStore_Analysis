# SuperStore Sales Analysis 

## Overview
The Superstore Sales Analysis project explores sales and profitability data to identify business trends, customer behavior, product performance, and regional patterns.
The goal of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives 🎯

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the best- and worst-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify factors affecting business profitability.
- Present insights through clear and informative visualizations.

## Key Areas of Analysis📊 
- Sales Performance
  -  Total sales
  -  Total profit
  -  Sales trends over time
  -  Monthly and yearly performance
-  Product Analysis
    -  Top-selling products
    -  Most profitable products
    -  Underperforming products
    -  Category and sub-category performance
-  Customer Analysis
    -  Customer segments
    -  Customer purchasing behavior
-  Regional Analysis
    -  Sales by region
    -  Profit by region
    -  Identification of high- and low-performing locations

## Tools Used 🛠️
- Python
- Pandas - Data cleaning and manipulation
- NumPy  -  Numerical analysis
- Power BI  -  Data visualization
- Jupyter Notebook – Analysis and documentation

## Project Workflow🔄
-  Data Collection: Load the Superstore dataset into the analysis environment.
-  Data Cleaning: Handle missing values, duplicates, incorrect data types, and inconsistencies.
-  Exploratory Data Analysis: Examine sales, profit, customers, products, categories, and regional performance.
-  Data Visualization: Create charts to identify trends, relationships, and performance patterns.
-  Insights & Recommendations: Summarize important findings and provide business-focused recommendations.

## Business Insights 🔍
-  From 2014 - 2017, superstore sales and profit has indicated an upward trend which means superstore sales and profit has increased year over  year
-  Technology is the highest category that generates more sales of 36.4% and profits margin of 47.12 in the product sale category.
-  over the years, superstore has made the highest sales and profit from the West Region
-  Central region made more sale than the south region but yet the south region has made more profits than the central region
-  Within these few years of sale transactions, Sean Miller is the top customer that patronize the store with the highest number of sales but the store has made the highest profit from another customer called Tamara Chand
-  The highest segment of customers patronizing superstore are consumers
-  Finally, increase discount usually reduced profitability for superstores
